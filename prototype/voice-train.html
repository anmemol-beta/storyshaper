Teach the story voice

Read each sentence aloud, the way you would at bedtime. About 100 short sentences (10 minutes) make the stories sound much more like you. You can stop and come back any time.

Can't read these sentences? Add videos or voice messages of them instead.

  • A quiet room, the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
  • Read naturally. Small mistakes are fine; just record that sentence again.
  • Press the space bar to start and stop on a computer.
