A parent's voice for story time

Add a short recording or a video of a parent. StoryShaper keeps only a 10-second clip and reads every story in that voice, even when they can't be there.

Step 1 · Whose voice is this?

The voice is used only inside StoryShaper, never exported, and every clip carries an inaudible watermark. You can delete it at any time.

Step 2 · Add a recording or a video

Any video or voice message works. One person talking calmly, with little background noise, gives the best result.
